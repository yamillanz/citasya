## Requirements
### Requirement: Mobile calendar hides internal scrollbar
The calendar component SHALL hide the internal scrollbar of the FullCalendar widget on viewports of 768px width or less.

#### Scenario: Viewing calendar on mobile
- **WHEN** the user views the calendar on a mobile device
- **THEN** no visible scrollbar appears inside the calendar area
- **AND** the calendar content remains accessible via swipe/touch scrolling
